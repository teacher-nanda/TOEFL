Speaking

Read a model answer

Click the 4 phrases that connect the ideas.

Tell me about a subject you enjoy studying. Why do you like it?

I really enjoy studying biology. The main reason is that it explains how everyday life works. For example, last year I learned how the human body fights infections, and I started to look at my own habits differently. Another reason is that my teacher made every class interactive, so I stayed curious. In short, biology is my favorite subject because it is useful and exciting.
Which part is the answer? the reason? the example? the wrap-up? Show me.

Vocabulary

Academic words

Say the word. Then answer the question: what does it mean? Click Show answer.

Check the meaning

  • What does deadline mean? the last day or time to finish something
  • What does assignment mean? a task from a teacher
  • What does require mean? to say that something is necessary
  • What does submit mean? to give work to a teacher
  • What does improve mean? to become better
  • What does benefit mean? a good result or advantage
  • What does challenge mean? something difficult that tests you
  • What does research mean? careful study to learn new facts
Your flashcards for these words are in our Quizlet class set. Practice them 5 minutes a day.
