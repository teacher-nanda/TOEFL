How Speaking Part 1 works

Click Next step to see how the task goes.

Step 1 · Listen. You hear a sentence once, then you speak.
Step 2 · Repeat. You say the same sentence right away. Exactly the same words.
Step 3 · Again. There are 7 sentences in this part. Each one is new.
Step 4 · Harder. The sentences start short and simple. Later they become longer and more complex.
Step 5 · Scored. Your voice is recorded and scored by AI. Nobody helps you, so you need strategies.
Today you learn the strategies, practice them, and then repeat 7 sentences with me.
Overview

What does the AI listen for?

Four things are scored. Which one is the problem in each situation? Choose. Then check.

1You say “The library close at ten tonight.”
2You stop for five seconds in the middle of the sentence.
3The last word is very quiet and nobody can understand it.
4You say every word, but with flat stress, like a robot.
5You change must to should.
Skill 1

Skill 1: remember meaning, not words

Your memory holds chunks (groups of words that belong together), not 12 separate words. Click between the words to mark the chunks. Then check.

Thelibraryclosesattentonight.
Pleasebringyourstudentcardtothefrontdesk.

A chunk is usually a short phrase: subject + verb, a place or a time, or the part before a comma.
Skill 2

Skill 2: find the key words

Key words carry the meaning: nouns, main verbs, numbers and times. Click them. Then check.

The lecture on climate science starts at nine o’clock.
If you miss the deadline, you must email your professor.
Say it aloud: make key words stronger and a little longer. Say small words (the, on, at, your) quickly and softly.

Correction

Hard sentence? Build it from the end

I say the last part. You repeat it. We add one part each time. After you repeat, click Add the next part.

by Friday afternoon
sign up by Friday afternoon
should sign up by Friday afternoon
Students who want to join the field trip should sign up by Friday afternoon.
The end of a sentence is the hardest part to remember. Practice it first, then add the beginning.

What we have learned

Today you learned six things for Speaking Part 1.

1
How Part 1 works
7 sentences. You hear each one once and repeat it exactly.
2
What is scored
Intelligibility, pronunciation and rhythm, fluency, grammar and vocabulary.
3
Chunks
Remember groups of words, not single words: The library closes / at ten tonight.
4
Key words
Stress nouns, main verbs, numbers and times. Say small words quickly.
5
Your mistakes
Missing word, changed word, wrong ending, word order. Name it, then fix it.
6
Build from the end
For a long sentence, practice the last part first. Then add the beginning.
