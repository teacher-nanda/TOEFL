Lesson 1 — Speaking Part 1: Listen and Repeat

Round 1 — sentences 1–3
  1. The library closes / at ten tonight.
  2. Please bring / your student card / to the front desk.
  3. The lecture on climate science / starts / at nine o’clock.
Round 2 — sentences 4–5
  1. If you miss the deadline, / you must email your professor.
  2. The new study room / on the second floor / is open / to all students.
Round 3 — sentences 6–7
  1. Students who want to join the field trip / should sign up / by Friday afternoon.
  2. Although the museum is free on Sundays, / visitors still need to reserve tickets online.

Slashes show the chunks. Read each sentence once at natural speed. The student repeats. Then the student clicks Show, compares, and marks Exact / Close / Missed.

Slide: Build it from the end (say each part; the student repeats)
  1. by Friday afternoon
  2. sign up by Friday afternoon
  3. should sign up by Friday afternoon
  4. Students who want to join the field trip should sign up by Friday afternoon.
Answer keys

What does the AI listen for? 1 Grammar and vocabulary · 2 Fluency · 3 Intelligibility · 4 Pronunciation and rhythm · 5 Grammar and vocabulary

Chunks: The library closes / at ten tonight. · Please bring / your student card / to the front desk. · If you miss the deadline, / you must email / your professor. · Students who want to join the field trip / should sign up / by Friday afternoon.

Key words: lecture, climate, science, starts, nine, o’clock · miss, deadline, must, email, professor

What changed? 1 Wrong ending · 2 Missing word · 3 Changed word · 4 Word order

Homework 2 (chunks in order): Please leave your bags at the front desk. · The professor will return your papers after the class on Thursday. · Because the bus was late, we missed the beginning of the lecture.

Homework 3 (key words): card, desk · lecture, science, nine · miss, deadline, email · join, trip, sign

Listen for while the student repeats
  • Missing or changed small words (the, a, to)
  • Word endings: -s, -ed
  • Stress on key words; steady rhythm
  • Silence in the middle of the sentence

Lesson 2 — Speaking Part 2: Take an Interview

Slides: Interview practice 1 and 2 (read each question aloud)
  1. How do you usually prepare for a big exam?
  2. Tell me about a teacher who helped you.
  3. Do you think students should work part-time while they study? Why?
  4. What is one skill you want to improve this year, and how will you practice it?

Listen for: a direct answer first, a reason, an example, a wrap-up, connectors, steady pace. Record every answer.

Answer keys

Question types: 1 Personal experience · 2 Opinion · 3 Habit or routine · 4 Future plan · 5 Personal experience · 6 Opinion

Answer structure: Answer → Reason → Example → Wrap up (the four sentences in that order).

Weak or strong: Answer B · It has a reason, an example and a wrap-up · A reason and an example

Connector jobs: The main reason is that = Reason · For instance = Example · However = Contrast · In short = Wrap up · Another reason is that = Reason · On the other hand = Contrast · To sum up = Wrap up · For example = Example

Keep talking: Well, let me think about that · What I mean is… · Another reason is that… · In short, …

Homework 2 (gap-fill): reason, example, instance, however, short, Another

Lesson 3 — Writing

Build a Sentence (key)

Practice 1

  1. The professor asked us to submit the report by Monday.
  2. Could you tell me where the registration office is?
  3. I have been studying English since I was twelve.

Practice 2

  1. The results of the experiment were published last year.
  2. She wanted to know if the library was open on weekends.
  3. If I had more time, I would join the debate club.

Homework 3 (typed sentences)

  1. Students must register before the deadline.
  2. I would like to know when the library opens.
  3. The article that we read last week was very useful.
Model answer parts: Position · Reason · Example · Reply to a classmate · Wrap up
Connectors: agree, however, instance, addition, result, conclusion
Make it better: 1 Many people like technology because it is useful in daily life. · 2 She has studied English for three years. · 3 For example, last month I finished a group project with students from four countries.
Feedback criteria (match the tracker)

Sentence structure · Organization · Relevance and development · Cohesion. Score each from 1 to 6 in the progress tracker.
