3. How your progress is measured

In the practice parts of each lesson, your teacher gives every answer a score from 1 to 5, and writes feedback: what you did well and what to improve.

ScoreWhat it means
5Clear and complete. Easy to understand.
4The meaning is the same. Only small slips.
3Mostly understandable. Some words or ideas are missing or unclear.
2Many important parts are missing.
1Very little can be understood.

After each lesson you receive a progress report (PDF) and a progress file. Keep the files. On the My progress page you can load them together and see your scores by lesson and by skill.
