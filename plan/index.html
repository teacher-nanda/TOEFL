1. The test today

The TOEFL iBT now has four sections (ETS content and structure, 2026 update):

SectionTasksItems
ReadingComplete the Words · Read in Daily Life · Read an Academic Passage50 (adaptive)
ListeningListen and Choose a Response · Listen to a Conversation · Listen to an Announcement · Listen to an Academic Talk47 (adaptive)
WritingBuild a Sentence · Write an Email · Write for an Academic Discussion12
SpeakingListen and Repeat (Part 1) · Take an Interview (Part 2)11 (7 + 4)

Each section and the overall score use a 1–6 scale in half-bands (the overall score is the average of the four sections). Writing and Speaking are scored by AI.

4. Lesson roadmap (COMPLETE track)

Click a lesson to open its details. Shorter tracks take the most important lessons from this list.

Lesson 1 · Speaking Part 1 — Listen and Repeat
GoalUnderstand how Part 1 works and repeat sentences accurately.
We teachHow the part works. What the AI scores. Chunking. Key words and stress. Types of mistakes. Building long sentences from the end.
PracticeChunk marking, key-word finding, mistake spotting, three rounds of 7 sentences read by the teacher (hidden from the student).
CorrectionExact / Close / Missed marking after every sentence. Back-chaining for the hardest sentence.
HomeworkRepeat with a practice video (starts at 2:26) and write the 2 hardest sentences with chunks. Rebuild sentences from chunks. Complete sentences with key words.
Progress evidenceResult screen: exact sentences out of 7, and which ones to practice. Baseline recordings.

Lesson 2 · Speaking Part 2 — Take an Interview
GoalAnswer interview questions clearly and develop each answer.
We teachHow the part works. Question types and the language each needs. The 4-part answer. Adding layers. Connectors. Phrases to keep talking.
PracticeQuestion-type sorting, ordering an answer, weak vs strong answers, layered answer, connector jobs, live interview questions with a timer.
CorrectionChoose one thing to fix, then redo the same answer. Answer bank for reusable answers.
HomeworkPlan and record an answer (with a video). Connector gap-fill. Rewrite a weak answer.
Progress evidenceBaseline recordings of 4 interview answers, scored on the 4 speaking criteria.

Lesson 3 · Writing — Build a Sentence + Write for an Academic Discussion
GoalBuild accurate sentences and write a clear, organized response.
We teachThe three Writing tasks. Grammar strategy for Build a Sentence. Parts of a discussion response. Connectors. Plan-write-check. Seeing the other side.
PracticeWord-order building, labeling the parts of a model answer, connectors, planning, timed writing, choosing the better sentence.
CorrectionSelf-check list. Edit task. Teacher feedback on the written response.
HomeworkWatch two videos and take notes. Write a discussion response. Type sentences from scrambled words.
Progress evidenceBaseline written response scored on the 4 writing criteria.

GoalLonger, more complex sentences with natural rhythm.
We teachLinking, word endings, numbers and names, sentences with clauses.

GoalWrite a clear email for a campus situation.
We teachPurpose, tone, polite requests, greeting and closing, clear details.
PracticeModels, ordering, rewriting informal to formal.

HomeworkOne email, a video, phrase bank.

Lesson 6 · Speaking Part 2 (II) — Take an Interview

5. Every lesson has the same shape

  1. Overview: how the task works
  2. Skills: short explanation, then an exercise to try it
  3. Practice: live speaking or writing with the teacher
  4. Correction: a tool to fix the main problem, then redo
  5. Result: what we have learned
  6. Homework: three pages, each a new type of exercise, nothing repeated

6. Progress checking

SkillCriteria we score (1–6, half-bands)
SpeakingIntelligibility · Pronunciation and rhythm · Fluency · Grammar and vocabulary
WritingSentence structure · Organization · Relevance and development · Cohesion (connectors)
  • Baseline: Lessons 1, 2 and 3 (recordings and a written response).
  • Checkpoints: Lessons 6 and 7, then Lesson 10.
  • Every lesson: Exact / Close / Missed marking in Part 1, and teacher notes on each answer.
  • Homework log: done or not done, and what was hard.

7. Homework system

  • Three pages per lesson. Each page is a different type of exercise, never a copy of the lesson exercise.
  • Each page contains everything the student needs. No page asks the student to go back.
  • Videos open at the exact point of the exercise when possible.
  • The next lesson starts by reviewing the homework.
